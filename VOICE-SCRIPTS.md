# Host voice scripts (five slots)

Voice direction: low, unhurried, refined, over-polite. He is delighted by his
own arrangements. Never shouting, never crude. A smile you can hear, and
underneath it, at the very end, something genuinely tender. Slight pauses at
the periods. Target format per REPLACE-AUDIO.md: MP3, mono, 44.1 kHz, filenames
exactly as below in public/audio/voice/.

## cold-open.mp3 (~20 s, plays at the first mark)

There she is. Awake at last, birthday girl. Happy thirty-third. I have
prepared the flat, the presents, and every unpleasant little interruption.
The first mark is waiting where you woke. Bring the camera. And Melissa...
do take your time. I certainly did.

## tape.mp3 (~55 s, narrates the seven stills in order; even pacing)

A cake for one. He baked it himself, which I found almost touching.

He sat where you sit now. He did not stay seated long.

He heard the same doorway you are hearing. He pretended that he did not.

He reached into the wall when the house told him to. Birthdays make brave
people of us all.

The balloons were my idea. He hated them. That is the only taste we ever
shared.

The clown offered him the candle. He would not take it. He said he needed
both hands free. For what, I have always wondered.

And there. The padlock gave its verdict. Five letters. He left you one
useful word, and it cost him everything he came here to win.

## draught.mp3 (~15 s, the flame dies)

Out. Just like that. One breath of moving air, and your little wish is
smoke. I am nearly sorry. I needed you to feel it go out once, so that
keeping it alive would mean something. Go and find another light. Nothing
in the dark behind you is new.

## trophy.mp3 (~18 s, the wish holds; the warmth starts here)

You made it to the wish. Thirty-three candles, one flame, and dead air.
You won. He never did. The box where you woke and the paper in the kitchen
are both yours now. The house is finished being cruel.

## present.mp3 (~18 s, the mask comes off; read the last sentence warm and plain)

Open it. I have known what is under that paper for weeks, and keeping the
secret nearly finished me. It sparkles, like the water it makes. Happy
birthday, Melissa. From the man who was never as anonymous as he pretended
to be.
