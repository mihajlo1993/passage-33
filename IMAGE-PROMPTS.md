# Image prompts

## Nanobanana prompts (generate, then drop into assets-incoming/ and run npm run generate:media)

### winner-padlock.png (optional epilogue still, 640x360 target)

A single frame from a degraded 1990s VHS home recording, heavy tape damage,
horizontal dropout lines, chroma bleeding, crushed blacks, timecode burned
into the lower right corner in thin monospaced digits. Subject: a tarnished
five letter combination padlock hanging open on a hasp, its letter wheels
spelling W I N N E R, lit by one warm tungsten source from the left. A sliver
of birthday cake with lit candles is out of focus in the background. Palette
limited to warm brown, dirty cream and dark green. Horizontal 16:9. No people,
no other text.

### sheet02.png (planter prop sheet replacement, A4 portrait, 1754x2480)

A scanned pencil and wax crayon drawing on slightly yellowed A4 paper, drawn
in a naive childlike hand: a potted plant with three broad leaves, drawn in
green crayon with heavy pressure, and next to it a small round valve wheel
drawn in grey. Beneath them, written in shaky block capitals with a black
crayon: IT CAN BE STOPPED. Light coffee stain in one corner, paper grain
visible, soft graphite smudges. Flat scan, no perspective, no hands, no
background objects.

## Crayon drawings for you to draw (kid-simple, 10 to 15 minutes each, A3)

Draw these badly on purpose; they are artifacts the previous guest left, not
art. Black plus one or two colours, heavy pressure, shaky caps.

1. THE CREATURE (corridor wall, replaces or joins prop sheet 01): a tall
   stick figure with too-long arms, scribbled black, one vertical line for a
   mouth running the whole head. Under it in block capitals: IT WATCHED ME.
2. THE FAN (kitchen, near the fan): a circle with three blades, grey or blue,
   with motion scribbles around it. Under it: SSSHHH. THE FLAME.
3. THE CAKE (with the sealed present): a lopsided cake with a forest of tiny
   candles, count them, exactly 33, in yellow and orange. Under it: FOR WHEN
   SHE WINS. This one lands after the reveal; make the 33 candles countable.
